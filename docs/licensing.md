!!! info "Commercial use and adaptations are welcome with attribution"

    You may copy, share, publish and adapt these images, including for commercial
    purposes. Credit **IPES Research Group**, link to the source page and the
    CC BY 4.0 license, and indicate any changes. Retain supplied copyright and
    attribution notices. Attribution must not suggest endorsement by the group.

For an unchanged figure, use a credit such as:

> Figure: IPES Research Group, *PyMHM*, [source page](https://ipes-lncc.github.io/pymhm/).
> Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

For an adaptation, use:

> Adapted from IPES Research Group, *PyMHM*,
> [source page](https://ipes-lncc.github.io/pymhm/),
> [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
> Changes: describe the modifications, for example cropping, translation or annotation.

Replace “source page” with the URL of the tutorial or gallery entry containing
the figure. Keep the credit with the image, in its caption or in an associated
credits section. The [license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en)
sets out the attribution conditions.

Third-party images, reproduced literature figures, institutional marks and other
assets with separate credits retain their stated licenses. This grant covers the
original documentation artwork; it does not assign a license to independently
created outputs of PyMHM users. The **software** remains licensed under
[LGPL-2.1-only](https://github.com/ipes-lncc/pymhm/blob/main/LICENSE).
